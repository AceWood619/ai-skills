# Knowledge Capture

Capture only durable, reusable knowledge; do not turn every interaction into a permanent artifact.

## Workflow

1. Identify the reusable problem, procedure, decision, caveat, evidence, and failure lesson.
2. Check the registry and project files for duplicates before drafting anything.
3. Produce a portable record with context, steps, assumptions, evidence, limitations, safety notes, tags, owner, and review date.
4. Redact credentials, personal data, secrets, and unrelated private content. Preserve source links and uncertainty.
5. Recommend compose, reference update, template, or new skill. State why and define an acceptance test.
6. Require review before installing, publishing, overwriting, or sharing the captured artifact.

## Guardrails

Capture is advisory. Never silently modify the skill registry, publish notes, infer approval, or preserve secrets. Do not claim a lesson is general until it has a clear boundary and reusable test.

## Output contract

Return the proposed artifact, provenance, duplicate check, promotion recommendation, safety review, owner, review date, and approval needed.

## Skip when

- The interaction is trivial, one-off, or not reusable.
- There is no concrete evidence, decision, procedure, or lesson to preserve.
- The user has not authorized storing or sharing the proposed record.
